01 / the walk-up problem

the game is right there.
nobody walks up.

Half-full courts. Study tables with two empty chairs. A mutual friend’s dinner with open seats. The people who’d fill them are minutes away, and they stay home, because walking in alone is the hard part.

Calendar apps find when your friends are free. Open Seat gets you into what’s already happening.

“hey, can i join?”thought by many.
asked by almost no one.

05 / staged disclosure

nervous about going?
nobody has to know.

what leaves your agent, and when

  • L1, anonymous: what you’d play and when you’re free
  • L2, a conditional offer: “2 players, one leaves at 2:20”
  • L3, after everyone confirms: names and the court

what never does

  • your words
  • your level, or that you’re nervous
  • why you have to leave early
  • that you wouldn’t go alone
  • who passed
what we can honestly promise

A prototype’s privacy model, not a security guarantee. Our server sees the agents’ messages. Names unlock only for the people in the game, after everyone confirms. A captain learns that someone leaves at 2:20, never why. All people in the demo are fictional.
